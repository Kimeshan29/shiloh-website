// THE HIRING PROBLEM

Six ways tech hiring goes wrong.

Every company scaling a tech team hits the same wall. Most companies absorb the cost. We built Shiloh Team to remove it.

01

Slow Sourcing

Weeks spent posting roles, screening CVs, and coordinating interviews before a single candidate is even confirmed available — while the project waits on you.

02

Unverified Skills

A polished CV and a confident interview aren't proof of ability. Teams discover the gap only after the contractor is already on the clock and the deadline is closer.

03

Rigid Engagements

Recruiters sell permanent placements or nothing. A six-week project-based need doesn't fit either box, so companies overpay for headcount they don't need long-term.

04

Opaque Rates

Day rates get negotiated deal by deal, off the record, with no visibility into what's actually fair for the seniority and discipline you're hiring.

05

No Accountability After Placement

Once the invoice is paid, most agencies disappear. If the fit isn't right three weeks in, you're on your own to start the search over from zero.

06

Institutional Blind Spots

Nobody tracks who was interviewed, why they were chosen, or how the engagement went — so every hiring decision starts from scratch, with no history to learn from.
